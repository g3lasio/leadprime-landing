Our Story

Built by contractors.
For contractors.

LeadPrime was born from Owl Fenc — a fencing and construction company in Northern California founded by Gelasio Sánchez and his son, Mervin J. Sánchez.

Gelasio and Mervin are Mexican immigrants and native speakers of Tsotsil, an Indigenous Maya language. They came to the United States and built a real construction business from the ground up — living every problem that contractors face: chasing leads, sending estimates, signing contracts, tracking licenses, and getting paid.

LeadPrime is the system they built to automate all of it. Not software designed by people who've never held a contract in the field — but by a family who ran the business first, and then built the tool they wished they'd had.

Today, LeadPrime helps Latino contractors, property managers, and real estate pros across the U.S. run their businesses in their own language — because we've been where you are.

LeadPrime is a product of Chyrris Technologies, the technology company the Sánchez family built on top of Owl Fenc's field experience.
