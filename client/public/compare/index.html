Price: what you actually pay to start

LeadPrime starts at $0 Pay-As-You-Go with $15 in welcome credits and no credit card — you pay only for what you use, and optional plans are $15/month (Pro) and $249/month (Network Elite). Jobber starts from about $39/month and scales with users. ServiceTitan does not publish pricing; public reports place it at $245–$500+ per technician per month, plus reported implementation fees of $5,000–$50,000. For a solo operator or a small crew, the difference in first-year cost is substantial.

Language: bilingual by design, not by translation

LeadPrime is built for Latino contractors in the U.S. — it works fully in English and Spanish, so the owner, the office, and the crew can all use it in the language they prefer. Jobber and ServiceTitan are English-first products; neither is built around a bilingual crew.

Native features: one platform vs add-ons

  • Estimates & invoices — native in LeadPrime; build an estimate on your phone and turn it into an invoice with one tap.
  • Digital contracts & e-sign (LeadSign) — included; clients sign on any device.
  • Payments — card and ACH built in (LeadPrime Pay).
  • AI agent (KEEN) — follows up on every lead, drafts messages, and books appointments, in English and Spanish.
  • License-verified B2B network — connect with verified contractors, property managers, and investors; neither competitor offers this.

One subscription that replaces 4–5

Beyond the CRM itself, LeadPrime bundles tools that are usually separate subscriptions: e-signature with AI signer and field mapping (LeadSign — the capability DocuSign gates behind its IAM Professional tier, ~$75/user/mo with a 3-user minimum, publicly reported), an AI contract generator that leaves the document ready to sign in the same flow (where Rocket Lawyer ~$39.99/mo and LawDepot ~$35/mo sell templates you fill in by hand), a government contract finder that matches SAM.gov opportunities to your trade (GovPrime), and license/insurance/W-9/workers'-comp tracking with expiration alerts (Business Health Passport). Time and cost figures are typical examples, not guarantees.

Who each platform is really for

ServiceTitan is enterprise software for large home-service operations — typically 20+ technicians — and its pricing and implementation reflect that. Jobber is a solid general field-service tool for small to mid teams. LeadPrime is built for solo operators and growing crews — especially Latino contractors, property managers, and real estate investors — who want estimates, contracts, payments, and AI follow-up in one place, starting at $0.
